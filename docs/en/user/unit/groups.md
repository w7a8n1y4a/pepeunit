# Unit groups

A `UnitGroup` gathers several `Unit` together for convenience. A group does not affect visibility or access, and only its creator sees it. One `Unit` can belong to several groups.

`Units` → `Groups`: the list of groups with a search by name.

Cell | What it does
-- | --
`Create group` | Enter a name, then `Create group`
`Name` | Changes the name of the group
`Icon` | Picks the icon of the group
`Members` | The `Unit` of the group. `Add` opens the `Unit` picker
`Delete` | Deletes the group, the `Unit` stay on the instance

A tap on a `Unit` among the members gives a choice: `Open` its menu or `Remove` it from the group.
