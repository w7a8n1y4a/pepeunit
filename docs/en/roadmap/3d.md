# 3D design system of enclosures

## Frame enclosures

### Basic elements

Designed for photopolymer printers

1. A standard grid pattern for cooling / ventilation
1. A range of frame sizes and intermediate ones
1. Standard proportions of all elements: chamfers | windows | frame thickness | frame width and length | terminal block sizes | holes

### Materials

Main materials:
1. Photopolymer resins
1. PETG, PLA and other plastics
1. Tempered and hardened glass
1. Epoxy (colored and opaque, scarlet, yellow, etc.) / Silicone / Rubber / Solder
1. Copper / Stainless steel / Brass / Titanium / Silver
1. Chemical deposition coating, with any of the available chemical elements

### Enclosure

Concept:
1. A rigid hollow frame, like an insulated glass unit, or a frame of any shape with various kinds of mounts
1. This rigid frame must have surfaces for laying glass / plastic plates / steel plates / films
1. Glass is attached mechanically / with epoxy
1. The empty space between the controllers and the enclosure is filled with epoxy / silicone / air
1. The frame can have any shape: half-frame / curved / L-shaped / Petals (when the frame is inside the device, a cross as a symbol of peace, etc.)
1. Between the glass panes there can be information plates / QR codes
1. Boards are mounted by soldering to the frame at the ground contacts / with tweezer clamps / by mechanical fixing
1. In the corners of the glass frames, round blind recesses should be made, they simplify mounting the glass

### Mounts to the outside world

1. DIN rail
1. Through holes, like on shipping containers, for fastening to each other or onto keys
1. MagSafe, for mounting on a magnetic insert
1. Dovetails: Spring-loaded | With a latch | With a double press
1. Ribbing | Round recesses | Medium recesses | Plain recesses | Grooves | Taper of the frames - for mounting to the outside world by friction or mechanically
1. Positioning in the only possible topological variant: Beveled corners | keys of various kinds | possibly a mechanism like the micro sd one

### Connectors

Depends very much on the build: waterproof, vibration-proof, etc.

1. Straight holes through the enclosure, for data wires, etc.
1. Connectors integrated into the outer boundary through an insulation layer
1. Terminal blocks like the Flipper's but with protruding connectors, they can be hidden with design elements so that they do not bend
1. Possibly an analog of mushroom-shaped retainers can be made, to keep the contact from breaking off
1. Standard flat terminal blocks for the main interfaces: Reset and flash jumpers / SPI / CAN / I2C / I2S / TYPE C / USB 2.0 / etc. They are fastened with concealed studs. All such blocks must have a version
1. A standard flat terminal block for specific MCUs, with versions, which would greatly simplify integration between devices of the same MCU
1. Standard reinforced connectors, for attaching external sensors

A variation from fully breathable to solid plastic and metal cubes that have terminal blocks, to which you can attach through special studs

### Power

1. Installing an 18650 in place of one of the frames
1. Installing an 18650 as an external element
1. Installing an 18650 as an additional module
1. From external sources over type c, for example
1. Installing smaller batteries in special enclosure extensions
1. Through terminal blocks
1. A quick swap of the 18650 is very much needed
    - like an sd card with a click, similar to fuses in old devices
    - a battery holder may be needed to make this work
    - it can be built into a desk, etc., a built-in type of enclosure, where only the connectors and the battery are exposed

### Cooling

1. By soldering ground wires to the board and to the outer frame
1. Through additional copper plates from the frame to the parts that heat up

## Casting machine

1. Vacuum chamber
1. Induction melting
1. Mold heating system
1. Mold cooling system through the bottom
